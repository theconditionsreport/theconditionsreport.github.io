The Forecast

19,685 feet on a mountain bike

Kilian Bron just rode a mountain bike down a 19,685-foot snow-covered summit in Bolivia — Huayna Potosí, a glaciated peak in the Cordillera Real — three years after a failed first attempt. That sentence alone should be enough to carry your Friday. Meanwhile, Matchstick Productions officially dropped the trailer for The Way Out, their 34th annual ski film, and the premiere tour is loading up. Warren Miller's Days Off trailer is live too, with Katie Burrell hosting the 77th edition. That's two major production houses with trailers out and dates announced — film season isn't approaching, it's here. Down south, Valle Nevado just ticked up to 546 centimeters. Portillo is holding at 269cm. The Andes winter of 2026 keeps writing history. NOAA's latest update puts the odds of a historic El Niño — stronger than any since 1950 — at 75 percent for this fall. Nine days until TGR's Actually, It Is Rocket Science premieres in Jackson Hole. Friday is stacked.
