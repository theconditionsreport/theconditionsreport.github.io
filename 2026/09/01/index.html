The Forecast

September arrives loaded

Sage Cattabriga-Alosa threading a spine line into a crevasse gap is the kind of clip that makes you set your coffee down and watch it three more times — and TGR just dropped it as a teaser from Actually, It Is Rocket Science, which hits the road September 19th. That's real, named, dated, and properly terrifying. Meanwhile, Indy Pass just announced it's adding 50-plus new resorts for 2026-27, blowing past the 300-resort mark and reopening sales after selling out last year — a genuine shot across the bow of the mega-pass industrial complex. The Andes refuse to slow down: Valle Nevado is still holding at 533 centimeters, Portillo at 295cm, La Parva at 251cm. Snow showed up on the high peaks of Western Canada over the weekend, because August had one more trick up its sleeve. September is here. Film season is live. The southern winter is fat. Let's go.
