The Forecast

Dinner at 3,600 meters

Valle Nevado is sitting on 533 centimeters of base and apparently decided that wasn't enough — now they're loading guests into snowcats at dusk, driving them up to the Ancla Summit at 3,600 meters, and feeding them dinner while the sun drops behind the Andes. That's the kind of thing you do when your season has been so historically deep that regular skiing just isn't showing off enough. Across the border, Las Leñas is holding at 234cm. Portillo is at 272cm. Meanwhile, two German climbers just survived a 65-foot plunge into a crevasse on Austria's Dachstein Glacier — one of them made a phone call from inside the ice, which triggered a 13-hour rescue that somehow ended with everyone alive. Colorado resorts are stacking up opening dates with Copper Mountain locking in November 6th, Red Bull just sent urban downhill riders screaming through Stuttgart's staircases, and the LINE crew dropped the trailer for A Luxurious Experience. Wednesday is serving.
