The Forecast

Two days to Level 1

Two days. Level 1 drops Thursday, and if you've been paying attention to the breadcrumbs, this one has teeth. Meanwhile, Newschoolers just dropped the Brand Battle edits — Line went absolutely unhinged at Sunshine Village and RMU made a serious case for a repeat at Whistler — and voting is live right now, which means the comment sections are already a warzone. Over at Aspen Highlands, the boot-packers are out stomping Highland Bowl into shape, one step at a time up 45-degree terrain, which is either the most thankless job in skiing or the most honest. Down south, Valle Nevado is still sitting on 566 centimeters like it's nothing, Portillo holds at 264cm, and the Andes winter of 2026 just keeps going. TGR and MSP are both on the road, premiere season is fully rolling, and Tuesday has no intention of being quiet.
