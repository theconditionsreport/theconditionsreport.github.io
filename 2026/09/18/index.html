The Forecast

One day to liftoff

Tomorrow night, the lights go down at the Walk Festival Cinema in Jackson Hole and TGR rolls Actually, It Is Rocket Science for the first time in front of a live audience. The teasers have been escalating all week — Todd Ligare's mondo front flip from The Vision, Keegan Kilbride's street segment, Maggie Voisin ripping through whatever equation TGR's been cooking up — and now it's one sleep away. Meanwhile, on the other side of the planet, the Snow League World Challenge kicks off today at Cardrona with a team-format halfpipe competition that's never been tried before: four squads, sixteen riders, freeskiers and snowboarders going head-to-head. And because the Southern Hemisphere refuses to quit, SnowBrains is calling for 50-70cm of fresh snow across the central Andes this weekend, with Nevados de Chillán potentially catching the lion's share. Valle Nevado still sits at 546 centimeters. The season down south is writing its own mythology. Friday is loaded.

The Edit

LINE Traveling Circus is back for Season 19, and the opener does what this crew has always done best: find snow in the most unlikely places and turn it into something joyful. Kale Cimperman leads a strike mission to his home turf of Pittsburgh — yes, Pittsburgh — after a snowstorm, then Will Wesson and Andy Parry hit their hometowns in New York before loading up for the Tell A Friend Tour. It's urban skiing, rail hunting, and the kind of infectious low-budget energy that's kept Traveling Circus the people's edit for nearly two decades. If you've been in this world long enough, this series is an old friend showing up at the door with a six-pack.
