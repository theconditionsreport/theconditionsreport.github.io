The Forecast

Five days to liftoff

Five days. That's how long until TGR rolls Actually, It Is Rocket Science into the Walk Festival Cinema in Jackson Hole for the world premiere — and the teaser clips are getting more unhinged by the hour. Teal Harle just dropped a Short where he mutters "should be no problem" right before things become very much a problem, and there's another clip of the crew chest-deep in Japanese powder that's basically a recruitment ad for quitting your job. Meanwhile, the X Games just announced a three-stop winter league with a full draft system — Aspen, Alberta, and a European finale in Finland — which is either the future of competitive action sports or the most chaotic experiment since the SuperPipe went to 22 feet. Down south, Valle Nevado is still sitting at 546 centimeters. Portillo holds at 264cm. The Andes are so deep they've stopped being a story and started being a geological formation. Premiere season is live, the Southern Hemisphere winter is historic, and Tuesday has your full attention.
