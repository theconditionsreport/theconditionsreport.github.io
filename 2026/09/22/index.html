The Forecast

TGR is on the road

TGR's Actually, It Is Rocket Science premiered in Jackson Hole last Friday night and is already rolling — two shows in Bend tonight, another in Salt Lake City, the Entourage crew ripping Mt. Hood in the latest teaser clip. The premiere tour is live and moving fast. Meanwhile, Portillo just crossed 1,000 centimeters of snowfall for the season. One thousand. That's 33 feet of snow at a resort carved into an Andean pass at 9,350 feet, and the base is still sitting at 279cm with more weather in the forecast. Valle Nevado is at 566cm. The Southern Hemisphere winter of 2026 is no longer historic — it's absurd. Three days out from the Matchstick Productions premiere of The Way Out, film season is fully loaded and the tour buses are fueled. Tuesday's got altitude.

The Edit

The North Face's Yard Time goes inside a maximum-security prison run club — a place where sport isn't recreation, it's survival. The film follows inmates running laps in circles, building discipline out of deprivation, showing up day after day in a system designed to break them down. It's raw, it's human, and it has absolutely nothing to do with skiing — which is exactly why it hits. Off-season or not, the mountain community understands what it means to find meaning in repetition, in showing up when conditions are brutal. This one earns every minute.

Off-Piste

🎬 Matchstick Productions premieres The Way Out on September 25 — three days from now. MSP's 34th annual ski film. Mark the calendar and find your local screening. powder.com

💰 Salomon is gunning for $3 billion in annual revenue. Parent company Amer Sports laid out an aggressive growth plan for the 79-year-old brand, pushing hard into trail running and lifestyle while keeping ski and snowboard at its core. The question is whether growth at that scale changes the product or just the marketing budget. snowbrains.com

🏠 Big Sky's school district just opened six new affordable homes for teachers and staff — 600 square feet each, built in Great Falls, trucked to a town where a studio apartment costs more than most mortgages. It's a small dent in the mountain-town housing crisis, but it's a real one. snowbrains.com

🐧 The Snow League partnered with a yellow-eyed penguin conservation project during the World Challenge at Cardrona. Team Challengers left more than a trophy behind in New Zealand — they helped fund protection for one of the world's rarest penguin species. thesnowleague.com
