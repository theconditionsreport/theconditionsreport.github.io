Film season drops tomorrow

Tomorrow. The first teaser trailers of the 2026 film season start dropping tomorrow — TGR is 37 days from premiere and counting down loud, and if last year's cycle is any indication, your feed is about to become a highlight reel of spines, sluff, and slow-motion face shots for the next three months. The southern winter is deep. The northern anticipation is deeper. We're in the corridor between hemispheres where everything is about to get very loud.

The Edit

Marianne Hogan went back to Western States for a third time knowing she needed to be better than she'd ever been — and she podiumed at the fastest edition in the race's history. Salomon TV's film follows Hogan and her crew through 100 miles of California's Sierra Nevada, capturing the unglamorous machinery of ultrarunning: the pacing, the suffering, the quiet negotiations with your own body at mile 80. It's a proper portrait of what it takes to compete at the sharp end of the sport's most iconic event.
