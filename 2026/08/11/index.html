The Forecast

The GOAT isn't done yet

Kelly Slater is 54 years old and just combo'd Gabriel Medina at Teahupo'o. Let that settle for a second. The wildcard drew a near-perfect heat in psycho surf, threading barrels that would make most pros in their twenties reconsider their career path — and he did it in the Round of 32 at the Outerknown Tahiti Pro. Meanwhile, the Andes keep doing Andes things: Valle Nevado is still sitting on 414cm, Portillo is at 284cm but its road has been buried for over 20 days — guests are literally helicoptering in — and Chilean ski officials are publicly berating the government to figure it out. Las Leñas just climbed to 241cm across the border. Film season teasers start dropping in four days, and the anticipation is building like a December storm cycle. The southern winter is fat. The northern summer is restless. And Kelly Slater apparently still has things to prove.
