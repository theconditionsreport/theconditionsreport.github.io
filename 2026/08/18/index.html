The Forecast

Seventeen feet deep and still counting

Valle Nevado just posted 516 centimeters. That's nearly seventeen feet of base depth — up over a hundred centimeters from where it sat two weeks ago, which already felt absurd. Portillo, riding the deepest season in recent memory, just announced it's extending operations into October because why would you close when the mountain is this fat. La Parva is at 264cm. Las Leñas is holding 234cm across the border. The Andes are having the kind of winter that makes Northern Hemisphere skiers physically uncomfortable scrolling their feeds in August. Meanwhile, film season teasers are officially live — TGR is in countdown mode, and the first clips are starting to hit. Passo Stelvio just shut down its summer operations because the Alps are melting, an activist investor is making noise at Vail Resorts, and Stab Magazine just declared they'll never run a live surf contest in the ocean. It's mid-August. The Southern Hemisphere is absolutely gorging itself.

Off-Piste

🏔️ Passo Stelvio, Italy's iconic summer ski area, has shut down operations amid record heat across Western Europe. The glacier pass, which normally runs May through November, couldn't hold on. Another reminder that summer skiing in the Alps is becoming a fragile proposition. Powder Magazine

📈 Activist investor Oasis Capital Management just increased its Vail Resorts stake by over 2.3 million shares, pushing its total holdings to a level that demands attention. The fund's play appears focused on operational efficiency and capital allocation — translation: someone thinks Vail is underperforming and wants the board to hear about it. SnowBrains

🐻 Pro trail runner Klaire Rhodes was attacked by a brown bear in Alaska on August 13 and walked away to tell the story. Her takeaway: "Bear spray works." File that under gear that earns its weight. Canadian Running Magazine

🧗 Tyler Thompson just clipped chains on Kinder Cakes 5.15a in Rifle, Colorado — his fourth route at the grade and second this year. The 5.15 club is still extremely small, and Thompson is quietly building one of the most impressive tick lists in American sport climbing. Gripped Magazine
