The Forecast

Cloudbreak goes live

Cloudbreak is on. The WSL Fiji Pro opened today with the world's best surfers threading one of the heaviest lefts on the planet, and Gabriela Bryan showed up ranked number one in the world, loose and dangerous and apparently not bothered by any of it. Meanwhile, meteorologist Chris Tomer just dropped his 2026-27 winter forecast and the headline is a screamer: a super El Niño, one of the strongest and earliest on record, with equatorial Pacific temps already running +1.8°C above normal. If that holds, the implications for the Western US snowpack are enormous. But we're getting ahead of ourselves — right now, the Southern Hemisphere is still gorging. Valle Nevado is holding at 516 centimeters. Portillo is running into October at 279cm. La Parva is stacked at 224cm. Hank Bilous just dropped a film about what style actually means in freeriding, Lindsey Vonn hit another recovery milestone after nearly losing her leg at the Olympics, and someone listed an entire ski area in Maine for $3.5 million. Tuesday is earning it.
