The Forecast

Film season is coming

The Andes are fat and getting fatter. Valle Nevado is sitting on a 368cm base — that's twelve feet of snow under your skis, the kind of mid-season depth that makes you forget it's July anywhere else on Earth. Portillo's at 269cm, La Parva at 226cm, and Las Leñas across the border is holding 208cm of its own. This is one of the best South American winters in recent memory, full stop. Meanwhile, the Northern Hemisphere is doing what it does in late July: burning. Wildfires are chewing through North Cascades National Park and the Scottish Cairngorms, because apparently no mountain range is safe from the smoke. But the real signal today came from Jackson Hole — TGR just dropped tickets for their 31st annual film tour, which means the countdown to film season has officially begun. Three weeks until teaser trailers start hitting. The smell of popcorn and chairlift grease is in the air.
