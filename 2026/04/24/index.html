The Forecast

The Storm Moved South

Friday morning and let's talk about the people who are still here. Not the ones who "love skiing" in December when it's easy, but the ones ordering a beer inside a literal ski factory in Denver, the ones studying the GFS models as a late-season storm slides into the Wasatch with 13 centimeters in the forecast for a mountain range most people wrote off three weeks ago. Utah just became relevant again, and Snowbird is paying attention. Mammoth picked up 17 centimeters this past week and is still running operations like it's February. Jackson caught 5 centimeters in the last 24 hours with another 6 coming. Meanwhile, Jay Peak is skiing trees this weekend — trees, in late April — with spring corn so dialed it should be illegal, and Killington's Superstar is doing its annual impression of a cockroach that will not die. Scandinavia has 11 centimeters in the three-day forecast and midnight sun. The map has shrunk but the pins that remain are the interesting ones, and Friday is the day to make a plan.
