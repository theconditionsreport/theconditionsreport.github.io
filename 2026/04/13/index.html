The Forecast

Two Days Out and the PNW Has a Pulse

Monday morning, two days to closing at the majors, and the Pacific Northwest just showed a heartbeat. Baker, Crystal, and Whistler have 46 centimeters — nearly a foot and a half — in the three-day forecast, which is the kind of late-April plot twist that used to be fiction. The sensor reads zero right now, so this is less of a top-up and more of a resurrection. Interior BC is also loading a 7-inch midweek surprise. Meanwhile, the rest of the map is doing what April does: the Rockies are scraped clean, Utah spent its Easter gift already, and the Sierra is coasting on last week's 26-inch haul with temps cold enough to keep things firm. The season's last act was supposed to be a quiet one. Instead, the Pacific is throwing punches. If you're anywhere within range of the Cascades, cancel Tuesday. Wednesday's going to taste like January.

Northeast thin
