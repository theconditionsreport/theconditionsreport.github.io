The Forecast

Dollar Days and Superyacht Ski Trips

Thursday morning and the season has reached the part where the price of a lift ticket tells you everything you need to know. Brian Head is charging one dollar tomorrow. One. That's less than the gas station coffee you'll drink on the drive down. It's also the perfect encapsulation of late-April skiing: the resorts that are still open aren't doing it for the revenue, they're doing it because the snow is there and the people want it. Elsewhere, the contrast is almost comical — someone is organizing a superyacht expedition to ski Antarctica for a price tag that could buy a small house, while the rest of us are scraping corn turns off whatever's left. Scandinavia picked up 32 centimeters in the last week with 14 more in the three-day forecast and the midnight sun is fully operational. Colorado got 20 centimeters in the last seven days. A-Basin is still A-Basin. Killington's Superstar is grinding moguls with the conviction of a religion. Jay Peak is skiing corn in the trees and having one of their best spring weekends. And Matchstick Productions is out in Whistler filming segments for the 2026 ski film, which means the people who make this sport's culture are already looking forward while the rest of us are still looking up at the snow line.
