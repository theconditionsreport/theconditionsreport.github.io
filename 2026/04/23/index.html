The Forecast

Mammoth Just Got Four Feet and Nobody's Leaving

Thursday morning and Mammoth Mountain just caught nearly four feet of spring snow, which is the kind of sentence that makes everyone who waxed their skis for storage last week feel personally attacked. The Sierra is running its own calendar now — Memorial Day is the target and nobody's blinking. Up in Wyoming, Jackson picked up 16 centimeters in the last 24 hours with another 8 in the three-day forecast, turning the Tetons into a legitimate late-April powder destination for the third time this week. Meanwhile, Vermont is out here playing the long game: Burke Mountain just got state approval to start snow farming, which is either visionary infrastructure or proof that New Englanders will fight the seasons with their bare hands. Killington's Superstar is still grinding. Jay Peak is still serving corn. Scandinavia has snow in the forecast. And if you're looking for the people who actually love this sport, they're the ones still out there right now — sunburnt, boot-tanned, and refusing to acknowledge that spring has an expiration date.
