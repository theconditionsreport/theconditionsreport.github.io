Happy 4/20, Here's Your Summer Ski Area

Monday morning, April 20th, and let's just acknowledge the date before we get into it — yes, it's 4/20, and if you know, you know. But here's something worth getting excited about that doesn't require a lighter: Beartooth Basin in Wyoming is prepping lifts for a summer opening next month, which means the season isn't ending, it's just moving uphill. The Sierra has 31 centimeters in the three-day forecast, which is Mammoth's cue to keep doing its thing. Out East, Killington's Superstar is grinding moguls into May with the single-minded focus of a dog that won't drop the ball, and Jay Peak is delivering spring corn so good it makes you wonder why anyone bothers with January. The Northern Rockies have 15 centimeters coming this week. Meanwhile, Powder just dropped nine hot takes from Natural Selection that are guaranteed to start arguments in every group chat from Bozeman to Breckenridge. The snow is thin in a lot of places. The skiing is not.

Rockies thin

Northern Rockies / Interior BC mixed

Jackson and Big Sky saw 17cm over the past week with 15cm in the three-day forecast and temps from 1°C to 8°C; BC got 15cm recently but nothing incoming — there's skiing to be had if you know where to look. →

Northeast spring

Temps are frigid at -11°C to -3°C with 7cm over the past week and 2cm coming — Killington's Superstar is grinding into May and Jay Peak is delivering some of its best spring corn of the year. →

Europe thin

Minimal coverage across the Alps with temps hovering around freezing and no meaningful snowfall in the forecast — glacier terrain at Zermatt and high-altitude zones are the only game in town. →
