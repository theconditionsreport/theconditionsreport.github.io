The Forecast

Six Days and a Sierra Storm

The calendar says six days until the big resorts shutter for the season, but the Sierra didn't get the memo. A legit storm cycle is bearing down on California this weekend — models are showing 1 to 3 feet for the higher elevations, which is the kind of late-season gift that turns a slushy April into a full-on reset. Everywhere else? The math is getting grim. Base depths across the West are functionally zeroed out, the Alps are clinging to glacier scraps, and Japan closed the book weeks ago. But the stories worth telling right now aren't about snowpack totals — they're about Vail Resorts pulling the plug on a Swiss resort while locals still have snow underfoot, MSP loading helicopters in Terrace for next year's film, and Natural Selection assembling its Alaska roster. Closing day is coming, but we're not there yet. Especially not in Tahoe.

Conditions Check

Pacific Northwest closed

Zero snow depth at Baker, Crystal, and Whistler with no new snow in the past week and nothing in the forecast — the PNW season is done. →

Rockies thin

Utah picked up 6 inches over the past week but base depths are essentially zero; Colorado is similar with temps pushing into the 50s — any remaining open terrain is extremely limited, though the Sierra is the bright spot with 12 inches forecast this weekend. →

Northern Rockies / Interior BC thin

Jackson and Big Sky show just 1cm of snow depth with 6 inches over the past week; Revelstoke sits at 2cm base with no new snow and nothing incoming — what's left is barely skiable. →

Northeast thin

Temps are cold enough at -5°C to 6°C with 2 inches over the past week and 35 km/h winds — Killington and Jay Peak may still have managed terrain open on snowmaking corridors, but natural coverage is effectively gone. →

Europe thin

The Alps are scraping by with 1-2cm base across France, Switzerland, and Austria; temps in the mid-30s to 40s°F with no new snow — glacier operations like Zermatt may hold on, but the broader season is winding down fast. →

Japan closed

Zero snow depth across both Hokkaido and Honshu with temps hitting 10-14°C and nothing in the forecast — Japan is done for the season. →

 

The Sierra's about to get a foot of snow with six days left on the clock. Sometimes the mountain gives you exactly what you need, exactly when you thought it was over.
