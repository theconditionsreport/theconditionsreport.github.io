The Forecast

The Mountains Don't Read Calendars

Thursday morning and the post-closing-day hangover is setting in across the West — except in the places that refused to close. Arizona Snowbowl just announced its second season extension, which is the kind of desert-mountain defiance you have to respect. The Northern Rockies are loading up 5 to 12 inches through Friday night before a warm weekend melts the evidence, so if you're within striking distance of western Wyoming or southwest Montana, today and tomorrow are the window. Jackson picked up 7 centimeters in the last 24 with another 6 in the forecast. Meanwhile, Alta is leaning into the pain with a "Best Worst Season" half-price ticket promo that's equal parts self-deprecation and marketing genius. Out East, Killington's Superstar is still grinding moguls and will be through May, and Jay Peak is carving spring corn through the trees like it's got something to prove. The season isn't winding down — it's just gotten selective about who's paying attention.
