The Forecast

Closing Day Is a State of Mind

Today's the day the calendar says it's over at most of the majors, and sure, a lot of gates are swinging shut for the last time. But here's the thing about closing day: it's an industry date, not a mountain date. The mountains are still out there. Cooke City is still buried in sled-access gold. Jay Peak is still carving corn through the trees. Killington's Superstar will be running moguls well into May. Utah has 16 centimeters in the three-day forecast — a parting gift nobody asked for but everybody deserves. Jackson's looking at another 11 centimeters by midweek. The people in costumes doing pond skims today earned every bit of that chaos, but don't confuse the party for the funeral. Spring skiing isn't the sad epilogue — it's its own chapter, and for those paying attention, it's barely started.
