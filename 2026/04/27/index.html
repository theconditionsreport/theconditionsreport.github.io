The Forecast

Blackcomb's Steepest Race and the Art of Not Dying

Monday morning and here's a question for you: when was the last time you raced down a 50-degree couloir with fifty other people? Because Blackcomb just did exactly that, and the footage is the kind of thing that makes your palms sweat through a phone screen. Meanwhile, the federal government quietly made ski instructor tips tax-free, which is either the most niche policy win in American history or long-overdue recognition that the people teaching your kids to pizza are criminally underpaid. On the snow side: Utah picked up 10 centimeters in the last 24 hours and Colorado grabbed 8 with another 6 in the three-day forecast — A-Basin and Snowbird are still earning turns in late April. Scandinavia is the quiet hero of the week with 24 centimeters in the last seven days and 12 more expected, all under the midnight sun. And in Hokkaido, the spring park season gets its final exclamation point with HiPOS 2026 at Nakayama Pass on Wednesday. The Northern Hemisphere is running on fumes and stubbornness, and honestly, it's never looked better.

The Edit

Matchstick Productions dropped "Confidence Over Skill" — a satirical take on the gap between ability and ambition that will hit uncomfortably close to home for anyone who's ever pointed it into terrain they had no business being in. Framed as an infomercial for "Disafear™," a fictional supplement that deletes your self-preservation instinct, the edit features MSP's pro roster demonstrating exactly what happens when confidence outpaces technique. It's funny, it's well-produced, and it's the kind of ski film that's actually about something — the culture of send-it-or-regret-it that runs through the sport like a fault line.

Northeast spring

No new snow and temps ranging from freezing to 13°C, but Killington's Superstar is grinding into May on man-made and natural coverage, and Jay Peak served excellent tree corn this past weekend — the East isn't done yet. →

Europe mixed

The Alps are warm (2–8°C) with minimal base across France, Switzerland, and Austria, but Scandinavia is the standout — 24cm in the last 7 days with 12cm more expected under midnight sun and temps around -5°C. →
