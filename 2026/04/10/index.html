The Forecast

Five Days to Last Chair

Five days out from closing weekend and the season is doing what it always does in April — splitting into two stories. The first one is about doors shutting: Mt. Bachelor just confirmed April 19, Loveland called it for April 26, and the dominoes keep falling across the West. The second story is the one you should actually pay attention to. The Sierra is loading up again — 8 inches in the last 24 hours with nearly 19 more expected through the weekend — and down in Utah, Brighton is flipping on the lights for two bonus nights of spring skiing starting tonight. Meanwhile, five American skiers just punched their tickets to the 2027 Freeride World Tour, which means the pipeline is healthy even if the snowpack isn't. The closing-day countdown doesn't have to be a funeral march. There are still turns to be had, cold ones to crack, and costumes to assemble. Go find them.

The Edit

Warren Miller's 1995 Colorado Snowsports Hall of Fame induction ceremony is a time capsule worth cracking open right now — five days before most of us take our last runs of the season. The man who turned 16mm film of himself skiing into a half-century empire talks about the early days, the obsession, and why he kept pointing a camera at people sliding down mountains. It's not a polished production piece — it's Warren being Warren, which is to say, more honest and funny than anything currently in your algorithm. Perfect viewing for a Friday night when you're already feeling nostalgic.

Off-Piste

• Mt. Bachelor officially closes April 19 — more than a month earlier than last year's May 26 shutdown. Historic low snowfall made this one inevitable, but president John Merriman says they're keeping spirits up through the final weekend. SnowBrains

• Yellowstone opens select roads April 17. The North Entrance (Gardiner) and West Entrance (West Yellowstone) go live first, with more gates following through May. If your spring touring plans include the park, start planning your approach. Unofficial Networks

• PSA from the Alps: stop walking on frozen lakes in April. European mountain resorts are practically begging tourists to stay off melting ice as spring temps rise. This should not require a press release, yet here we are. SnowBrains

• Martigny, Switzerland is going for the world's largest raclette again — not the biggest wheel, but the most people eating raclette simultaneously. Last year's record: 4,893. If you're in Verbier and want to melt cheese competitively, this is your calling. InTheSnow
