The Forecast

Two Frenchmen Just Broke Mont Blanc

Wednesday morning and two French alpinists just did something unreasonable on Mont Blanc. Mathéo Jacquemoud and Samuel Equy set a new ski mountaineering FKT from the church in Chamonix to the summit and back — a round-trip that redefines what human legs and lungs can do at altitude. That's the news worth leading with today: not which resorts are closing, not who's packing it in, but the fact that people are still doing absurd, beautiful things in the mountains while most of the ski world is scrolling real estate listings. Elsewhere, Scandinavia picked up 31 centimeters in the last seven days with more in the forecast and the midnight sun turning everything gold. A-Basin caught 19 centimeters in the last week and still has terrain worth driving for. Killington's Superstar is open. Jay Peak is skiing corn in the trees. And in Austria, the spring parks at Kitzsteinhorn are running setups that would make a January park rat jealous. The season isn't ending. It's doing something more interesting than that.
