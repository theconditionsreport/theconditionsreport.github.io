The Forecast

Seven Days to Closing

Seven days. That's what's left at most major resorts before the lifts stop spinning and the costumes come out for closing day. The snow data across North America reads like a eulogy — base depths cratering, no new snow in the forecast, temps pushing above freezing everywhere that matters. But here's the thing: this is the week you remember. The sunburned goggle tan. The 11 a.m. corn run that peels off the surface like butter. The parking lot beers that start at noon because nobody's pretending anymore. Natural Selection just dropped its Alaska lineup for 2026, spring deals are keeping lifts turning for the diehards, and somewhere in Switzerland a mountain is literally cracking apart. It's April. Everything is ending and everything is perfect.

Conditions Check

Pacific Northwest closed

Baker and Crystal are done — 11cm over the past week wasn't enough to hold base — temps hovering 27–40°F with no new snow in the forecast. →

Rockies thin

Utah saw 26cm over 7 days and Colorado got 10cm, but base depths are negligible at the sensor stations — temps pushing 35–50°F in the Wasatch and 27–47°F along the Divide with nothing incoming. →

Northern Rockies / Interior BC thin

Jackson and Big Sky sitting on barely 1cm of base with 15cm over the past week but 50 km/h winds and temps up to 42°F chewing through what's left; Revelstoke at 2cm base with nothing in the pipe. →

Northeast thin

Jay Peak and Killington picked up 5cm over 7 days with cold temps (7–29°F) helping preserve what's on the ground — limited terrain still possible at the holdout mountains, so check resort pages before writing off your season. →

Europe thin

The Alps are running on fumes — 1–2cm base across Chamonix, Verbier, and St. Anton with temps in the mid-30s to upper 40s°F and no snow in the forecast; Scandinavia saw 9cm over 7 days but base is near zero with 52 km/h winds battering Riksgränsen. →

Japan closed

Zero base across both Hokkaido and Honshu with temps hitting 41–58°F — the Land of the Rising Sun has moved on to cherry blossoms. →

 

Seven days until the ropes go up for the last time. Ski like you mean it.
