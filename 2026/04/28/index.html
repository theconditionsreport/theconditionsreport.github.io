The Forecast

60% of America Is Dry and 15 Resorts Don't Care

Tuesday morning and the drought monitor just dropped a number that should make every mountain town sit up straight: over 60% of the contiguous United States is currently dry, and the snowpack deficits from a lackluster Western winter are about to become everyone's summer problem. But here's the thing about this sport — it doesn't quit when the math gets ugly. Fifteen resorts across the country are still running lifts right now, from Mammoth's seemingly immortal operations to A-Basin catching 3 inches in the last 24 hours at elevation. Colorado picked up 13 centimeters in the last week. Utah got 23. Scandinavia dropped 30 centimeters in seven days under the midnight sun and has more coming. Killington's Superstar is doing what Superstar does — grinding moguls into May with religious conviction. And in Åre, Sweden, Jesper Tjäder is building another physics-defying rail course for Unrailistic's third edition this week. The season is smaller, yes. But the people still in it are the people who were always going to be in it.

Europe spring
