The Forecast

Heavenly Rises from the Dead

Saturday morning and the season just did something it wasn't supposed to do: Heavenly is back. The Tahoe resort that closed weeks ago is reopening today and tomorrow after that mid-April storm dumped enough to justify one last run at it — limited terrain, 10-to-5, no questions asked. Meanwhile, Sugarloaf in Maine is pushing to April 26, Jay Peak is serving up some of its best spring corn of the year this weekend, and Killington's Superstar is doing what Superstar does — grinding moguls into May with the quiet determination of a machine that doesn't know how to stop. The sensor data across most of the West reads zeros, which tells you nothing about what's actually happening on the ground. A-Basin is still spinning. Mammoth is still holding. And over in Sölden, 50,000 people just showed up for an EDM festival on a glacier, which is either the future of ski resorts or the end of civilization, depending on your perspective. The season isn't dying — it's shapeshifting. The people still chasing it this weekend are the ones who always understood that.
