The Forecast

The U.S. has a freeride team now

U.S. Ski and Snowboard just announced its first-ever freeride roster — a legit national team, built for the FWT and the 2030 Olympic stage, and stacked with names who've been earning it on the qualifying circuit for years. That's not preseason noise, that's a structural shift in how America treats big-mountain skiing. Meanwhile, TGR dropped its Haines, Alaska segment from "Actually, It Is Rocket Science" — Colter Hinchliffe, Tim Durtschi, and Anna Tedesco threading massive spines between chess games and snowmobile river crossings — and early-season snowpack numbers out West have gone fully unhinged: one Colorado basin is sitting at 15,300% of median. That's not a typo, that's October flexing. LINE Traveling Circus just rolled Episode 19.2, Ride Snowboards dropped "Red Ceiling" with Jed Anderson, and Newschoolers kicked off Season 5 of NS Originals with Wrecka himself. Film season is wide open. Friday has range.

TGR's Haines, Alaska segment from "Actually, It Is Rocket Science" is the kind of big-mountain filmmaking that reminds you why you watch ski films in the first place.
