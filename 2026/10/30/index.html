The Forecast

A-Basin opens mid-week and nobody saw it coming

Arapahoe Basin just pulled the ultimate Wednesday surprise — doors open, lifts spinning, free sandwiches at the top, and hardly a soul in the lift line because nobody saw it coming. Colorado's 26/27 season is officially live. Meanwhile, the Freeride World Tour dropped its full 2027 calendar for a landmark 20th season: six stops from Baqueira Beret in Spain to the finals at Verbier, with a brand-new stop at Snowbird slotted into the mix. Mount Fuji just got its first snow of the season, nearly a month late, which tells you something about the state of things over there. Snowboard Mag's Hot 100 board test issue hits shops tomorrow — 100 boards, 15 testers, a full season at Mammoth. Banff Mountain Film Festival World Tour kicks off Sunday. The season is not coming. The season is here. Friday has receipts.

Off-Piste

🏔️ Team USA and The North Face officially unveiled the new kits for U.S. Ski & Snowboard athletes — race gear, training apparel, and a consumer collection coming soon. Eight-year deal. The freestyle team shots are chef's kiss.

🏔️ Mount Fuji just got its first snow of the season on October 30 — nearly a month behind its average date. Japan's most iconic peak finally wearing white, but the delay is another data point in a warming trend.

🏔️ Winter Park's long-dreamed gondola connecting the resort to the town faces a public vote by November 3. The ballot asks voters to form a regional transportation authority and authorize up to $120 million in borrowing. Decades in the making.

🏔️ Ski Cooper is saying goodbye to president and CEO Dan Torsell after 14 years running the independent Colorado ski area. Leadville's mountain loses a quiet, steady hand.
