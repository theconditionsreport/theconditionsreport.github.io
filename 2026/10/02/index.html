The Forecast

Levi opens today

Levi Ski Resort in Finnish Lapland opens today — October 2, alpine and cross-country, on snow they banked from last winter and kept under tarps all summer like a squirrel with a trust fund. It's the first European resort to fire up lifts for 2026-27, and the snow is real, even if the birch trees haven't gotten the memo yet. Across the Atlantic, Aspen Snowmass is about to unwrap its own stockpiled stash for a free rail park this weekend — 85% of the snow volume survived under mats, and they're handing out laps for nothing. Meanwhile, Newschoolers Brand Battle voting is live and getting hostile: 4FRNT dropped their edit from Utah and RMU's Whistler entry is already up, 40 challenges apiece, tattoos were involved, and the comment sections are doing what comment sections do. Down south, Valle Nevado is sitting at 569 centimeters like it's just a normal thing. The season is opening from both ends now.
