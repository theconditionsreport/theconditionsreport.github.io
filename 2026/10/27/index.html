The Forecast

Carrick-Smith makes British history at Sölden

Karl Fostvedt just picked up IF3 Male Skier of the Year off the back of his work in TGR's 'Actually, It Is Rocket Science' — a nod that felt inevitable if you watched his segments, and one that lands right as film season is in full swing. Meanwhile, Freddy Carrick-Smith finished 12th in the Sölden GS opener on Sunday — the best result by a British male in a World Cup giant slalom in the modern era, and the kid is 19. Mt. Bachelor woke up to its first real dusting of the season over the weekend, Oregon's snowline is dropping, and Rome Snowboards just dropped the trailer for 'Bedlam.' Down south, Valle Nevado ticked up to 724 centimeters — that's nearly 24 feet of base — and still shows no signs of closing. Banff Mountain Film Festival World Tour kicks off Saturday. Tuesday has momentum.

Off-Piste

🏔️ Colorado's first avalanche accident of the season hit Sunday on Mt. Bross — a skier triggered a large wind slab at high elevation and was buried with their head under the snow. Partners dug them out in about five minutes. Early-season snowpack is deceptive; if you're touring, bring the gear and bring partners who know how to use it. SnowBrains

🎬 Rome Snowboards dropped the trailer for 'Bedlam' — chaos, pandemonium, hubbub, and all the other synonyms for what happens when Rome's team gets loose. Another film-season entry to add to the queue. Whitelines

🎬 TGR just released the trailer for 'Eureka,' Owen Smith's 15-minute film spanning Austria, France, Germany, and Spain with Cooper Watson, Nathan Cyr, Charlie Burnham, and James Klein. Jackson Hole premiere is November 1 at the Center for the Arts — same day Banff kicks off and Killington makes its annual bid for first chair in the East. TGR / YouTube

🏔️ Cranmore Mountain Resort in North Conway, New Hampshire, has completed the first four phases of its base-area buildout, adding ski-in/ski-out lodging and modernizing its infrastructure. More phases are planned — the Northeast is quietly investing. Unofficial Networks
