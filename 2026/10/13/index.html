The Forecast

Another foot stacking in the Cottonwoods

Utah's Cottonwood Canyons are getting buried right now — another foot is possible before the system clears out Thursday, and all nine Wasatch resorts are in the crosshairs. Meanwhile, Mt. Rose just fired up the snowguns with base-elevation temps dropping hard over the weekend, making its annual bid to be Tahoe's first resort spinning lifts. Colorado, Utah, and California are all in line for more mountain snow next week as remnants of Hurricanes Rachel and Simon collide with a cold front sweeping the West. A-Basin and Loveland are two days out from their traditional opening-day knife fight. Down south, Valle Nevado is sitting at 701 centimeters — that's over 23 feet — and still running lifts, because the Andes winter of 2026 is simply allergic to endings. Red Bull Rampage got shut down by wind before the men's finals could finish, Warren Miller premiere season is about to kick off Thursday, and Tuesday is very much alive.
