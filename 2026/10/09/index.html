The Forecast

Valle Nevado plans to overlap with Colorado openings

Valle Nevado just announced it's staying open through November 8 — the latest closing date in the resort's history — which means Chilean lifts could be spinning on the same day A-Basin or Loveland fires up first chair in Colorado. Let that sink in: hemispheres overlapping, winter to winter, no gap. The base at Valle Nevado is 691 centimeters. That's nearly 23 feet of snow. Portillo's at 361cm, La Parva at 259cm, and the Andes winter of 2026 simply will not quit. Meanwhile, fresh snow dusted the Alps overnight — peaks across Austria, France, Italy, and Switzerland going white down to 1,600 meters — and Faction's film tour premiere is tomorrow. Salomon Snowboards just dropped the teaser for Shiver Creek, a two-year global project, and the FWT revealed a 2027 ski roster that reads like a greatest-hits compilation. Friday has layers.
