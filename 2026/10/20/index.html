The Forecast

Slavmatic drops tonight in Prague

Level 1's Slavmatic premieres tonight in Prague — Simon Bartik's short film following the Czech freeski scene from muddy urban rails with barely any snow to the manicured kickers of Kimbo Sessions, and it's exactly the kind of scrappy, culture-first filmmaking that makes October feel alive. Meanwhile, POWDER just pulled the curtain on its 2026/27 cover — Sam Smoothy and Jossi Wells shot by Jasper Gibson somewhere steep enough to make your palms sweat — and Killington dropped a $280 million decade-long transformation plan that includes a 30% snowmaking capacity boost. Valle Nevado is still sitting at 706 centimeters of base. That's over 23 feet, and they're still spinning. The Andes winter of 2026 is writing its own mythology at this point. Tuesday has premiere energy.

The Edit

Simon Bartik's Slavmatic is a love letter to the Czech freeski scene — a world with no real mountains, barely any snow, and zero excuses not to make it work anyway. The short film follows a crew from muddy urban rails and improvised hits back home to the perfectly sculpted park at Kimbo Sessions, and the contrast is the whole point. It premieres tonight at Prague Freeski Premiere 8, and it's the kind of grassroots, culture-first filmmaking that Level 1 has always been the right home for. No helicopter budgets, no Alaska spines — just the people and the obsession.

Off-Piste

🏔️ Toggenburg Mountain in Central New York is set to reopen after a four-year legal battle that started when the resort shut down in 2021 after nearly 70 consecutive years of operation. The courts have cleared the path, and the hill that once had five lifts and 24 trails is coming back to life — good news for a region that needs every vertical foot it can get. SnowBrains

🎿 Kimberley Alpine Resort in BC just had new lift towers installed for its updated Tamarack quad chairlift — the first of four new chairs going in across Resorts of the Canadian Rockies properties. The $32M+ multi-resort upgrade is on schedule for the 26/27 season. Powder

🌊 Dave Stanfield, the voice of professional surfing for over 40 years, has passed away. If you've ever watched a WSL webcast or sat in the sand at a CT event, you've heard his call. A four-decade run behind the mic, always with a smile and a lightness that matched the sport he loved. Surfer

🧊 Will Gadd — the Canadian ice climbing legend — just went ice climbing in Australia's Snowy Mountains with Hugh Newall, finding fragile lines and marginal conditions. Because when you're Will Gadd, you don't wait for the ice to come to you. Gripped
