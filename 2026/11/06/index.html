The Forecast

Snowguns roar to life in New Hampshire

Bretton Woods just lit up the snowguns — Halloween night temps dropped hard enough for New Hampshire's largest ski area to fire its full arsenal, and the photos look like a midwinter postcard if you squint past the calendar. Aspen is getting buried this week with top-to-bottom coverage across town and mountain, the kind of November white-out that makes locals start waxing in the garage. Sam Klein just landed the cover of Snowboard Mag's Issue 23.1 with a boat-to-beam shot out of Buffalo that Mike Basher captured, because of course the best snowboard photo of the season came from western New York. TGR's Bute Inlet segment is live — Kai Jones, Karl Fostvedt, Teal Harle, and Maggie Voisin running heli laps on some of the biggest spines in the Coast Mountains. Banff Mountain Film Festival World Tour rolls on. Valle Nevado is still sitting at 734 centimeters. That's over 24 feet. The Andes just refuse to acknowledge seasons. Friday has snowguns and spine lines.
