The Forecast

Canada jumps the gun — Alberta opens early

Lake Louise just opened a full week ahead of schedule, Norquay bumped up to Friday, and Nakiska is spinning chairs by the 7th — Alberta said forget the calendar, we're skiing now. Meanwhile, the Girl Winter Film Tour is 50 stops deep and filling rooms from Montreal to the West Coast with the kind of community-first energy this sport needs more of. Korua quietly dropped 'Clouds,' a full film built around Nicholas Wolken chasing surf waves in the Japanese mountains — frozen water is still water, after all. Valle Nevado ticked to 734 centimeters of base. That's over 24 feet. The Andes refuse to quit. Down in the Philippines, Gabriel Medina just won his first CT in three years at Cloud 9, which has nothing to do with skiing but everything to do with the feeling of watching someone remember exactly who they are. Tuesday has early-season receipts and a Canadian accent.
