The Forecast

Tuckerman Is Still Tuckerman and That's the Problem

Saturday morning and Tuckerman Ravine just reminded everyone that spring skiing's greatest amphitheater doesn't hand out participation trophies. A brutal collision — caused by an out-of-control skier on one of the Ravine's steeper pitches — is making the rounds, and it's the kind of footage that should be required viewing for anyone who thinks hiking an hour into a glacial cirque means the rules don't apply. The incident is a sharp counterpoint to the general vibe right now, which is this: the season is a candle burning from both ends, and the people still chasing it are doing so with purpose. Scandinavia picked up 26 centimeters in the last week with 13 more in the three-day forecast, making it the last true snow machine in the Northern Hemisphere. Colorado got 20 centimeters in seven days and A-Basin is still turning. Killington's Superstar is grinding spring moguls into May like it always does. Jay Peak is skiing corn in the trees and having a genuinely good weekend. And if you're wondering where else to point your car, Powder just published their May hit list — the answer is fewer places than January, but the ones that remain are earning it.

Europe mixed
