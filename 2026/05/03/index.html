The Forecast

500 Skiers Walk Into a Swedish Hillside

Sunday morning and 500 people just pointed their skis downhill at the same time in Åre, Sweden — Red Bull Homerun's 15th anniversary looked like a medieval siege with better outerwear — and if that doesn't tell you everything about where the energy lives right now, check the forecast: northern Colorado is looking at 10–15 inches midweek, and A-Basin just extended operations through May 10 on the strength of 14 fresh inches. That's not a resort limping to the finish line; that's a resort that got a second wind and decided to sprint. Scandinavia continues to be the last real snow machine on the planet, picking up 29 centimeters in the last week with another 12 in the three-day forecast under temps that are still solidly below freezing. Meanwhile, the spring touring window in Japan's Tateyama is producing the kind of 3,000-meter ridge runs that make the whole shoulder season worth it. The map is shrinking. The quality of what's left is not.
