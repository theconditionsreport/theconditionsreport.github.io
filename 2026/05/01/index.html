The Forecast

Unrailistic Is Live and the Season Refuses to Die

Happy May Day. Jesper Tjäder's Red Bull Unrailistic goes live today in Åre, Sweden — a rail contest so architecturally ambitious it makes most park setups look like furniture assembly — and Scandinavia is backing it up with 28 centimeters in the last week and 13 more in the three-day forecast. Meanwhile, Colorado got 20 centimeters in the last seven days and both A-Basin and Copper Mountain are sending it into closing weekend with fresh snow on the ground and the kind of send-it energy that only exists when the clock is running out. Snowbird just announced a May 10 closing date, giving Utah one more full week of spring turns. Killington's Superstar Showdown — a bell-to-bell endurance race dreamed up by Jim Ryan and Madison Ostergren — sold out in record time for May 16, which tells you everything about the appetite for skiing right now. And at Mt. Hood Skibowl, a chairlift maintenance accident killed one worker and injured another yesterday, a grim reminder that the infrastructure holding this sport together demands as much respect as the mountains themselves. The season is small. The intensity is not.
